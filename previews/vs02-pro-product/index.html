Efficient Cooling Without Idling the Engine

The VS02 PRO uses an R410A dual rotary compressor with a 12V/24V DC inverter drive — pulling cabin temperature down fast while keeping current draw within a single auxiliary battery bank.

Rooftop unit engineered for long-haul cab cooling

  • 12,000 BTU rated cooling capacity, ample for sleeper cabs and standard RV layouts up to 80 sq ft.
  • Inverter-driven dual rotary compressor adjusts power to real-time demand, reducing battery drain.
  • Whisper-quiet operation at ≤45 dB — designed for overnight rest periods, not just daytime use.

Twin brushless condenser fans

BLDC motors deliver high airflow at low noise, sustaining steady cabin temperatures even at +40 °C ambient with the engine off.

Slim indoor panel, directional airflow

Adjustable louvers route cold air toward the bunk or the driver seat. LED indicators and a wired remote keep operation simple for fleet drivers.

Built to Last on Every Route

  • UV-stabilized ABS cover resists harsh sun, road grit and pressure-wash exposure.
  • EPDM foam gasket under the rooftop unit insulates the cabin and shields the cutout from water ingress.
  • Zinc-coated condenser fins withstand coastal humidity, salted highways and dusty mine roads.

Sealed cable entry & pre-terminated harness

Pre-routed wiring with strain relief and a sealed grommet makes a clean install on the cab roof — no improvised holes, no exposed copper.

Pre-shaped mounting plate

Bottom chassis is machined to mate with the gasket and roof studs — fewer shims, less vibration, easier first-time installs for new installer accounts.

Battery-Powered, Off-Grid Ready

  • Runs from the auxiliary 12V or 24V DC battery bank — no inverter, no shore power required.
  • Up to 8–10 hours of overnight cooling on a 200–300 Ah LiFePO4 bank.
  • Built-in undervoltage, overvoltage, overcurrent and overtemperature safeguards protect both the AC and the battery.

Eco & sleep modes

Lower-current modes trade peak cooling for steady, quiet overnight operation — extending runtime when the truck is parked off-grid for the night.

Battery protection logic

The controller monitors the bank voltage in real time and steps power down before damaging the cells — important for fleets running shared chargers and mixed cycling patterns.

Quiet Operation, Better Rest

  • ≤45 dB on standard mode, ≤32 dB in sleep mode — quieter than most rooftop RV AC units.
  • Four spring-isolated compressor mounts absorb vibration and road shock.
  • Indoor air path is acoustically tuned to keep airflow strong without harsh whistle.

Aerodynamic low-profile cover

Streamlined shell reduces wind noise at highway speed and lowers the impact on truck fuel economy versus boxy retrofit AC units.

Tuned indoor air path

The transition between the rooftop unit and the indoor panel is shaped to reduce turbulence at high fan speeds — drivers hear airflow, not the AC.

Lightweight, Low-Profile, Compact

  • Approx. 30 kg / 66 lb total system weight — within the spare-roof budget on most semi cabs and RVs.
  • Low-profile rooftop unit keeps overall vehicle height in check, useful for low-bridge routes and garage parking.
  • Compact footprint leaves room on the roof for solar panels, antennas and roof racks.

Compact chassis

Strategic cutouts in the chassis reduce weight without sacrificing rigidity, and improve airflow across the condenser.

Low-profile fan housing

Twin shrouded fans keep the rooftop unit short, blending with the cab line for a clean retrofit look on dealer demo vehicles.

Smart Control, Effortless Comfort

  • Wired remote with eco, sleep, fan-only and turbo modes for everyday driver use.
  • Self-diagnostic alerts flag undervoltage and overtemperature events before they turn into warranty calls.
  • Cabin and evaporator dual sensors hold a steady setpoint across the night — no hunting between hot and cold.

Driver-friendly controls

Large buttons, clear icons and a backlit display so drivers can adjust the AC mid-rest without fumbling with a phone app.

Service-ready diagnostics

Fault codes map directly to the installer service sheet — making it easy for first-time dealers to triage warranty cases on the lot.

3-Step Install

Trained installer can mount VS02 PRO in under 2 hours

1

Prep the roof & opening

Confirm the cutout falls inside the allowed installation window (510×380 mm min · 800×490 mm max), clean the perimeter, then apply the foam gasket.

2

Secure the rooftop unit

Drop the chassis onto the gasket, attach the four threaded rods to the base, align the mounting plate from inside and torque evenly.

3

Wire, test & mount panel

Run the pre-terminated harness to the auxiliary bank with the inline fuse, mount the indoor panel, then run the commissioning checklist before handover.

Dealer & Installer FAQ

Short answers to the questions our B2B inquiry desk hears most often. For project-specific cases, request a quote and we will route to the right product engineer.

How much battery capacity is needed for overnight cooling?

A 200 Ah LiFePO4 bank at 12V (or 100 Ah at 24V) typically supports a full sleep-mode night in a sleeper cab. For RVs and vans with extended off-grid use, plan for 300 Ah at 12V (or 150 Ah at 24V).

Does VS02 PRO fit a standard 14 × 14 inch RV roof cutout?

Yes — VS02 PRO mounts directly on a standard 14 × 14 inch RV cutout, and also supports a wider installation window (510 × 380 mm minimum to 800 × 490 mm maximum) for retrofit on truck cabs and custom upfit jobs.

What is the MOQ for dealer and wholesale orders?

Sample MOQ is 1 unit (paid). Dealer/wholesale tier starts at 20 units; private-label and OEM programs are reviewed per project. Pricing and lead times are provided in writing with every quote.

Do you support 12V and 24V from the same SKU?

The VS02 PRO is offered in dedicated 12V and 24V SKUs, plus a dual-voltage option that auto-detects bank voltage on power-up — useful for dealers stocking a mixed fleet customer base.

Which certifications are available?

CE, EMC, RoHS and Reach files are available on request. Country-specific certifications (UKCA, SASO, INMETRO, etc.) can be discussed during the quote stage based on the destination market.

How is the unit packed for export?

Each unit is sealed in a foam-lined export carton with desiccant. Standard pallets ship 20 / 40 units depending on configuration. Pallet photos and packing list are part of the quote pack.
