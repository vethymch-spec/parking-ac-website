## Open Datasets — CoolDrivePro

Free datasets published under CC-BY-4.0. Cite as: CoolDrivePro (2026).

### parking-ac-spec-database
- 9 parking AC units across 6 manufacturers (CoolDrivePro, Dometic, Webasto, RigMaster, Indel B, Carrier)
- Fields: BTU, voltage, compressor, refrigerant, COP, noise, weight, price, warranty
- [JSON](./parking-ac-spec-database.json) | [CSV](./parking-ac-spec-database.csv)

### us-anti-idling-laws-by-state
- All 50 US states + DC
- Fields: 2026 diesel avg price, anti-idle law (Y/N), max idle minutes, fine range, temperature exemptions
- [CSV](./us-anti-idling-laws-by-state.csv)
