Vehicle fit and access

Mark the case outline on the cargo floor and simulate opening the lid. If the fridge will move between vehicles, check that there is room to grip the handle in each vehicle. The catalog lists separate vehicle and household cords.

Do the carton measurements give the installation size?

Use 580 × 330 × 295 mm for the cabinet. The larger 652 × 395 × 385 mm figure describes the shipping carton.

13 L or 18 L car refrigerator: which fits your vehicle?

The documented 13 L cabinet is narrower, while both documented 18 L variants are lower. Use the available width, height and lid clearance to decide. Liters describe internal capacity and cannot establish vehicle fit.
