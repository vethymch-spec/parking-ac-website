Vehicle fit and access

The 13 L case is 44 mm narrower and 20 mm taller than the listed 18 L cases. Measure the narrowest door opening as well as the intended position. Request the approved restraint method for the vehicle.

How much narrower is it than the 18 L cabinet?

The listed widths are 286 mm and 330 mm respectively. Allow additional space for cables, access and the operating clearances in the manual.

Related buying guides

13 L or 18 L car refrigerator: which fits your vehicle?

The documented 13 L cabinet is narrower, while both documented 18 L variants are lower. Use the available width, height and lid clearance to decide. Liters describe internal capacity and cannot establish vehicle fit.

What to check before powering a car fridge overnight

Measure energy used over a representative night and check supply compatibility separately. A fridge’s running wattage, battery nameplate capacity and an ECO-mode label are each insufficient to predict overnight runtime.
