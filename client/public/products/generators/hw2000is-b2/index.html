Related buying guides

AC inverter generator or DC charging generator?

Choose AC when the load expects AC at a specified voltage and frequency. Choose a DC configuration only after matching the receiving equipment, charge controls and voltage range. The same power number can describe two incompatible outputs.

Rated watts vs peak watts on an inverter generator

Size sustained loads against rated output. Maximum output is a separate short-duration figure. Check the permitted duration and the appliance starting demand before counting on that extra capacity.
