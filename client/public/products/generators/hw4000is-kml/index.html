HW4000iS-K/M/L AC inverter generator

HW4000iS-K/M/L is an AC inverter-generator family with 3.0 kW rated output and 3.3 kW maximum output. The catalog lists a 149 cc engine.

Output and configuration

The catalog gives the same 149 cc displacement for several models in this housing family. Use the full generator model and the continuous output rating to identify the correct unit on a quotation.

Does a 149 cc engine identify the 3 kW version?

Several models in the table use a 149 cc engine. Check the HW4000iS model prefix and the 3.0 kW rated-output line together.

Rated watts vs peak watts on an inverter generator

Size sustained loads against rated output. Maximum output is a separate short-duration figure. Check the permitted duration and the appliance starting demand before counting on that extra capacity.
