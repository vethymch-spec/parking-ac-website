Output and configuration

For a 12 V battery project, first obtain the receiving battery’s permitted charge profile and current. Identify any external charge controller and request a wiring diagram from the supplier before selecting this unit.

Can I use the DC output as an AC outlet?

This configuration is listed as a DC generator. A suitable AC supply requires separately specified equipment.

Fuel-powered portable generators must be operated outdoors. US CPSC guidance on generator placement and carbon monoxide.

AC inverter generator or DC charging generator?

Choose AC when the load expects AC at a specified voltage and frequency. Choose a DC configuration only after matching the receiving equipment, charge controls and voltage range. The same power number can describe two incompatible outputs.
