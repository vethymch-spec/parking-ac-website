Selection notes

The pack’s calculated nominal energy is 5.12 kWh. An installer still needs the permitted depth of discharge and inverter losses to estimate energy delivered to household loads. Request a mounting drawing and wall-loading requirements for the proposed location.

Is the 5 kWh figure the available AC energy?

It is a capacity class. Multiplying the nominal voltage by the amp-hour rating gives 5.12 kWh before operating limits and conversion losses.

Home battery kWh vs kW: which number sizes backup?

Use kWh to estimate how long a system can supply energy. Use kW to check whether the inverter can carry the appliances that run together. A backup design needs both numbers.
