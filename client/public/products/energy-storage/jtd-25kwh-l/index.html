JTD-25kwh-L wheeled home battery

The 25 kWh-class wheeled LiFePO₄ cabinet is rated at 51.2 V and 460 Ah. That corresponds to approximately 23.55 kWh of calculated nominal energy.

Selection notes

Compared with the 30 kWh-class wheeled cabinet, the listed first dimension is 145 mm shorter. Verify the orientation on the installation drawing before checking a doorway or turning space.

Is the 25 kWh model the same cabinet as the 30 kWh version?

The catalog lists 595 × 450 × 680 mm for this cabinet and 740 × 450 × 680 mm for the 30 kWh-class version.

Home battery kWh vs kW: which number sizes backup?

Use kWh to estimate how long a system can supply energy. Use kW to check whether the inverter can carry the appliances that run together. A backup design needs both numbers.
