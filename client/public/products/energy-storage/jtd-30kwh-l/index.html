JTD-30kwh-L wheeled home battery

The 30 kWh-class wheeled LiFePO₄ cabinet is rated at 51.2 V and 560 Ah. That corresponds to approximately 28.67 kWh of calculated nominal energy.

Selection notes

At 740 mm on the first cabinet dimension, the delivery route deserves an early check. Request shipment weight, doorway orientation and floor-loading details for the exact installation.

How much energy does 560 Ah represent?

At the listed 51.2 V, it gives about 28.67 kWh nominal. For usable AC energy, request the discharge window and efficiency of the matched inverter.

Home battery kWh vs kW: which number sizes backup?

Use kWh to estimate how long a system can supply energy. Use kW to check whether the inverter can carry the appliances that run together. A backup design needs both numbers.
