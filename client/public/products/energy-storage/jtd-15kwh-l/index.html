JTD-15kwh-L wheeled home battery

The 15 kWh-class wheeled LiFePO₄ cabinet is rated at 51.2 V and 280 Ah. That corresponds to approximately 14.34 kWh of calculated nominal energy.

Selection notes

The 270 mm cabinet depth can matter in a narrow utility room. Check the delivery route and the space required for connectors and service access separately.

How does the 15 kWh class relate to 280 Ah?

At 51.2 V nominal, 280 Ah gives about 14.34 kWh of nominal energy. Use the model-specific usable-energy figure for a backup-duration estimate.

Home battery kWh vs kW: which number sizes backup?

Use kWh to estimate how long a system can supply energy. Use kW to check whether the inverter can carry the appliances that run together. A backup design needs both numbers.
