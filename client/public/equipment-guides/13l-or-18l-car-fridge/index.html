13 L or 18 L car refrigerator: which fits your vehicle?

The documented 13 L cabinet is narrower, while both documented 18 L variants are lower. Use the available width, height and lid clearance to decide. Liters describe internal capacity and cannot establish vehicle fit.

Compare the actual exterior dimensions

The 13 L cabinet measures 560 × 286 × 315 mm. The two 18 L cabinets measure 580 × 330 × 295 mm. Going to 18 L adds 20 mm to the first dimension and 44 mm to the second, while reducing cabinet height by 20 mm. A shallow overhead shelf and a narrow gap between seats can therefore lead to different choices.

Measure the route into the vehicle

Mark the footprint with tape or cardboard. Check the narrowest cargo opening, seat position, lid swing, room for the plug and the clearance stated in the manual. A cabinet that fits on the floor can still be difficult to load, vent or lift. Keep the driver’s controls and normal movement clear.

Choose the handling features

The 13 L description shows provision for a strap. The hidden-handle 18 L model offers a recessed grip, while the glass-panel 18 L version lists touch controls and ECO mode. Ask how each candidate is to be secured in the vehicle and request the correct accessory list.

Keep carton size on the shipping line

The 13 L carton is 625 × 350 × 398 mm. The listed 18 L carton is 652 × 395 × 385 mm. Use these numbers for freight planning and cabinet dimensions for fitment. For a fleet trial, photograph the marked-out space and the power connection before ordering the sample.
