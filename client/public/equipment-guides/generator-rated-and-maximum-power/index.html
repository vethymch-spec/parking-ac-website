Rated watts vs peak watts on an inverter generator

Size sustained loads against rated output. Maximum output is a separate short-duration figure. Check the permitted duration and the appliance starting demand before counting on that extra capacity.

Read the two rows on the same model

The HW3000iS-K/M/L catalog entry lists 2.5 kW rated and 2.8 kW maximum. A 2.7 kW continuous load exceeds its rating, even though it is below the maximum figure. The HW4000iS-K/M/L entry lists 3.0 kW rated, which changes the continuous-load comparison.

Build a load list before adding headroom

As an illustration, a 900 W tool, an 800 W appliance and 200 W of lighting total 1,900 W if all run together. Against a 2,500 W rated unit that leaves an arithmetic difference of 600 W. That difference says nothing about how the generator responds to a starting motor. Obtain the starting requirement and check whether multiple devices can start together.

Compare equivalent configurations

Ask each supplier for rated and maximum output on the actual voltage, frequency and outlet version. Record how long peak output is permitted and the conditions under which the ratings apply. Where the manual requires a reduction for site conditions, use the adjusted rating in the comparison.

Turn the calculation into a sample test

Give the supplier your normal load sequence and the hardest expected start event. Agree on what will be measured and recorded during a trial. Use the result for the tested configuration and retain the generator and appliance nameplate photos with the report.
