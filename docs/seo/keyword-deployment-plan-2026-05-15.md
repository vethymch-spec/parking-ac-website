## Low-Fit Or Exclusion Queue

These terms should not become primary product pages unless CoolDrivePro has matching inventory or verified support content:

- AC compressor / condenser replacement terms: `12v ac compressor`, `electric ac compressor 12v`, `freightliner cascadia ac condenser`, `truck ac condenser`.
- HVAC contractor terms: `shenandoah air conditioning & heating`, `hvac freightliner`, `hvac van` unless used in support or negative keyword work.
- Diesel heater terms: `webasto 12v diesel heater` should map only to heating-comparison content if needed, not parking AC product pages.
- Weak product-fit novelty terms: `12 watt ac`, `12 volt personal air conditioner`, USB cooler phrases, and room/home AC phrases.

## Internal Linking Rules

- Core 12V page links to RV, van, rooftop, mini split, and products.
- Vehicle pages link back to existing truck, RV, van, no-idle, battery-runtime, and fitment hubs.
- Portable-intent pages must explain the difference between evaporative/personal coolers and compressor-based parking AC.
- Competitor pages must not claim superiority without sourced, current specs.
