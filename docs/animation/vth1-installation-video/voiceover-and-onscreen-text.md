# Voiceover And On-Screen Text

Primary market: US  
Tone: clear, practical, calm, non-technical  
Voice: friendly installer, not a sales announcer

## Full 90-120 Second Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-Screen Text | Production Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6s | "This is the finished VTH1 rooftop parking AC installation." | `CoolDrivePro VTH1` | 开头先给最终效果, 参考短视频首帧策略。 |
| 6-12s | "The rooftop unit sits outside, while the ceiling outlet delivers cool air inside the cabin." | `Rooftop unit + interior air outlet` | 外机和内饰出风口都要出现。 |
| 12-20s | "Before installation, do not look for a fixed sunroof size. Measure the clear roof opening on your vehicle." | `Measure the clear roof opening` | 这是全片核心纠偏。 |
| 20-32s | "Measure the usable opening after trim or hatch parts are removed." | `Clear opening = usable roof cutout` | 不要说某个车型天窗。 |
| 32-44s | "VTH1 fits clear roof openings from 20.1 by 15.0 inches to 31.5 by 19.3 inches." | `Fits: 20.1 in x 15.0 in to 31.5 in x 19.3 in`<br>`510 x 380 mm to 800 x 490 mm` | 英寸主标, 毫米副标。 |
| 44-50s | "If the opening is smaller than the minimum size, it must be enlarged before installation." | `Too small`<br>`Enlarge opening` | Red state. |
| 50-54s | "If the opening is within the range, the VTH1 is ready to install." | `Fits VTH1`<br>`Ready to install` | Green state. |
| 54-58s | "If the opening is larger than the maximum size, use an adapter plate before mounting." | `Too large`<br>`Adapter plate required` | Yellow state. |
| 58-68s | "Clean and dry the roof surface around the opening, and avoid roof reinforcement beams." | `Clean surface`<br>`Avoid roof beams` | 用简化警示图标即可。 |
| 68-78s | "Apply the sealing foam evenly around the opening, with no gaps at the corners." | `Apply sealing foam evenly` | 密封泡棉要连续闭合。 |
| 78-90s | "With help, lower the rooftop unit straight down and align it with the opening." | `Lower straight down`<br>`Align with opening` | 不展示危险单人搬运。 |
| 90-102s | "From inside, install the mounting frame and tighten the bolts evenly in a cross pattern." | `Install inner frame`<br>`Tighten evenly` | 交叉顺序用 1-2-3-4 高亮。 |
| 102-112s | "Connect the DC power cable with fuse protection near the battery, following the product manual." | `Fuse protection near battery`<br>`Follow the manual` | 电路只做简化示意。 |
| 112-120s | "Turn on the VTH1, check cool airflow, and confirm the roof seal is secure." | `Test cooling`<br>`Check airflow and sealing` | 最后回到最终舒适画面。 |

## Copy Rules

- Use `clear roof opening`, `roof opening`, or `roof cutout`.
- Do not use `standard sunroof size`.
- Do not imply every vehicle opening fits directly.
- Keep inch labels larger than millimeter labels in the US version.
- Keep technical warnings short and visual.
