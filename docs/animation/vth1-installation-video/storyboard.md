# VTH1 Installation Animation Storyboard

Target length: 90-120 seconds  
Primary version: US English, inch-first labels  
Secondary labels: millimeters below or beside inch values

## Story Principle

The customer should understand one thing before installation starts: the VTH1 is not matched to a fixed sunroof name. It is matched to the measured clear roof opening range.

## Timecoded Storyboard

| Time | Scene | Visual Direction | Key Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6s | Finished result | Interior cabin view. VTH1 ceiling outlet panel is already installed. Cool air particles flow downward. Camera slowly pushes in. | Show the final result first. |
| 6-12s | Product hero | Exterior 3D view of a truck/RV roof with the VTH1 installed. Clean white background or light workshop environment. | VTH1 rooftop parking AC. |
| 12-20s | Roof opening concept | Roof panel becomes semi-transparent. Existing hatch/trim lifts away to reveal a rectangular clear roof opening. | Measure the clear roof opening. |
| 20-32s | Measurement | Tape measure and dimension arrows appear across the opening. Labels show width and length. | Do not rely on a fixed sunroof name. Measure your opening. |
| 32-44s | Fit range frame | A green adjustable rectangle expands from minimum to maximum range over the roof opening. | Fits 20.1 in x 15.0 in to 31.5 in x 19.3 in. |
| 44-58s | Three fit states | Split screen or carousel: red too small, green fits, yellow too large. Keep the green state centered longest. | Too small: enlarge. Fits: install. Too large: adapter plate required. |
| 58-68s | Prepare roof | Clean roof surface around the opening. Show cloth wipe, remove dust/water, avoid roof beams indicator. | Prepare a clean, flat sealing surface. |
| 68-78s | Sealing foam | Sealing foam gasket animates around the opening as a continuous loop. Slight compression preview. | Apply sealing foam evenly around the opening. |
| 78-90s | Lower unit | Rooftop unit descends vertically into position. Use alignment arrows and a soft lock-in motion. | Lower the VTH1 straight into the opening. |
| 90-102s | Inner mounting frame | Interior view. Mounting frame aligns, bolts insert, torque sequence highlights in cross pattern. | Tighten mounting bolts evenly. |
| 102-112s | Electrical connection | Simplified battery, fuse, DC cable, and unit connector path. Add fuse near battery callout. | Connect protected DC power according to the manual. |
| 112-120s | Test and finish | Switch on. Blue airflow from outlet. Exterior roof water-seal check icon. End on installed product and brand. | Test cooling, airflow, and sealing. |

## Optional Short Cutdown: 45-60 Seconds

If a shorter social video is needed, keep only:

1. Finished result.
2. Measure clear roof opening.
3. Fit range and three fit states.
4. Seal, lower, tighten, connect.
5. Cooling test.

The fit range scene must stay in every version.
