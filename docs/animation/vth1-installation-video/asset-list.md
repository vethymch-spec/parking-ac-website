# Asset List

The production does not require detailed STP-level engineering models. Use simplified, clean, dimensionally believable models that explain the installation clearly.

## 3D Models

| Asset | Detail Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| VTH1 rooftop exterior unit | Medium | Accurate silhouette and product proportions are more important than internal parts. |
| VTH1 top fan grille | Medium | Place toward the rear section of the rooftop unit, based on the product drawing. |
| VTH1 underside/ceiling outlet panel | Medium | Needed for the opening and closing shots. Should look clean and finished. |
| Vehicle roof section | Low-medium | Generic truck/RV roof panel, slightly curved edges acceptable. |
| Clear roof opening | Parametric | Rectangular opening that can scale to too small, fit, and too large states. |
| Removed hatch/trim | Low | Used only to explain that measurements happen after trim/hatch removal. |
| Sealing foam gasket | Medium | Continuous rectangular loop, slightly compressible material. |
| Inner mounting frame | Medium | Interior frame/press plate aligned to opening. |
| Bolts and washers | Low-medium | Four or more bolts depending on final product manual. Use generic if exact count is unconfirmed. |
| DC cable | Low | Red/black or sheathed cable path from unit to fuse/battery diagram. |
| Fuse and battery | Low | Simplified diagram assets only. |
| Cool airflow | Graphic | Blue translucent airflow particles or ribbons. |

## Graphic Elements

- Dimension arrows for width and length.
- Minimum opening frame: `20.1 in x 15.0 in` / `510 x 380 mm`.
- Maximum opening frame: `31.5 in x 19.3 in` / `800 x 490 mm`.
- Fit-state badges:
  - Red: `Too small` / `Enlarge opening`.
  - Green: `Fits VTH1` / `Ready to install`.
  - Yellow: `Too large` / `Adapter plate required`.
- Warning icons for roof beams, power disconnect, and fuse protection.
- CoolDrivePro logo and final URL.

## Material Style

- Product shell: satin white or light gray with subtle bevels.
- Product top-view proportion: approximately 925 mm front-to-back length by 891 mm left-to-right width.
- Rear fan grille: circular grille near the rear half of the top shell.
- Roof panel: neutral light gray metal.
- Interior panel: clean white with dark vent details.
- Sealing foam: matte black or dark gray, slightly compressed after unit placement.
- Measurement graphics: white lines with blue accent, plus color states.
- Background: light workshop or clean neutral studio.

## Brand Feel

- Practical, clean, and confidence-building.
- More instructional than flashy.
- Product should look durable and modern, but the measurement graphics must remain the hero.
