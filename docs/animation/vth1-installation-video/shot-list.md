## Camera And Motion Notes

- Treat the vehicle front as the camera-side/front label direction in top-down shots. The VTH1 925 mm product length runs front-to-back, and the top fan grille is in the rear section.
- Use top-down orthographic views for measurement scenes, so the opening and labels are readable.
- Use soft, slow motion for product hero shots.
- Avoid fast spins, excessive lens distortion, and complicated exploded views.
- Keep labels locked to screen space or large enough to read on mobile.
- Render safe margins for 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 crops.

## Label Priority

1. `20.1 in x 15.0 in` minimum.
2. `31.5 in x 19.3 in` maximum.
3. `510 x 380 mm` and `800 x 490 mm` secondary.
4. Red/green/yellow fit-state labels.
5. Installation action labels.
