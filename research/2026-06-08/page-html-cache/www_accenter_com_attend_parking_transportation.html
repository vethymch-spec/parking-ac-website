Parking & Transportation

Parking

There are 1,200 parking spaces on the first level of the Center (Garage height 7') for attendees and exhibitors. In addition, numerous public lots and garages are within walking distance of the Center. Please see appendix for a map showing ancillary parking areas.

Due to fire code restrictions, parking on loading docks, ramps or on the exhibit floor is not allowed. Vehicles in violation will be towed at the owners' expense.

Parking at the Convention Center is $20.00.

ParkMobile

Don’t leave home without knowing where you’re going to park. Reserve your parking ahead of time for every event at the Atlantic City Convention Center. Drive directly to the lot and have a guaranteed spot waiting for you. Park Mobile allows you to make a reservation in app or visit parkmobile.io to reserve a lot near the Atlantic City Convention Center today.

Motor Coach Parking

Motor Coach parking is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Transportation

By Rideshare

 Let Lyft be your ride to the action! As the Official Rideshare Partner of Atlantic City Convention Center, we're here to make getting to and from the event easy.

New to Lyft? Use code ACCONVENTION for 50% off your first 2 rides (up to $10/ride). 

*Subject to Lyft Terms

By Rail

NJ TRANSIT is New Jersey's public transportation corporation. The Atlantic City Rail Terminal is adjacent to the Atlantic City Convention Center, just a few blocks from the boardwalk. Travel between Philadelphia and Atlantic City is easy and convenient. Plan your trip to Atlantic City today!

By Air

The Atlantic City International Airport is located 12 miles outside of Atlantic City and is serviced by Spirit Airlines. The Atlantic City International Airport (ACY) conducts commercial and general aviation operations, offering air travel to support commerce, tourism, and the general public. ACY provides convenient service to Atlantic City and the Southern New Jersey shore region and outbound travel to a host of outer market destinations. It is the designated airport for visitors to Atlantic City—the region's premier entertainment destination. www.acairport.com

The Philadelphia International Airport is located 60 miles from Atlantic City and is serviced by over 30 airlines. Philadelphia International Airport (PHL), the only major airport serving the nation's 6th largest metropolitan area, is a large hub airport serving 32.24 million passengers annually. Twenty-five airlines, including all major domestic carriers, offer nearly 500 daily departures to more than 140 destinations worldwide. The Airport is easily accessible and convenient to many tourist sites, business centers, and cultural hubs. www.phl.org

Jitneys

The Atlantic City Jitney Association offers affordable, quality transportation for all your events.

Since 1915, AC Jitney has been serving the greater Atlantic City area, including all your favorite shore points in Central and South Jersey! AC Jitney offers affordable, quality transportation for any event, big or small! From birthdays and prom nights, to bachelorette parties, wine tours, and weddings, AC Jitney can help make your big days -- and nights out -- fun, affordable, and memorable!
