Lovely hotel choice
Overall a very pretty, modern hotel with great service. I stayed over the weekend (Thursday night - Monday morning) arriving at about 11:30 pm on Thursday and leaving around 4:30 am on Monday. I experienced a small hiccup with the hotel transportation I had arranged for the night of my arrival, but other than that, my stay was great. As a platinum member, I was upgraded to a bigger room which is always nice. From what I understand, all rooms have ceiling to floor windows which is always breath taking. The rooftop pool is beautiful and wasn't packed either of the two times I enjoyed it. I highly recommend the buffet breakfast in the mornings. It's a little pricey at around ~$15 USD but definitely worth it. There have plenty of food options, a station to order eggs, omelets, pancakes, Mexican breakfast dishes, etc. In addition, they have plenty of juice options and iced and infused coffee.

Really such a modern, posh place to stay and such a deal if you're able to stay on Marriott points.

Misc. Info: parking is located under the hotel in a parking garage and has Telsa charging stations (if that's helpful to any Telsa drivers). There are not many restaurants / coffee shops within walking distance, but a quick Uber can get you to a really nice restaurant zone. We ate a La Casa de Los Platos but there are several cute places in that area.

Enjoy your trip to GDL!
