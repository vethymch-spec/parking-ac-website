    Frequently asked questions
    What are lithium power solutions for trucking?

    Lithium truck power systems provide clean, quiet power for HVAC, appliances, and electronics during rest periods, helping fleets reduce idling, fuel costs, and emissions.
    How do lithium APUs compare to diesel APUs?

    Lithium systems are quieter, lower maintenance, more fuel-efficient, and emission-free while delivering reliable onboard power.
    What is a Battle Born power station?

    Battle Born power stations are portable lithium systems that provide plug-and-play AC and DC power for work, recreation, and emergency backup applications.
    How long can a lithium truck power system run?

    Runtime depends on battery capacity and energy usage, but many systems support overnight HVAC use and electronics without idling.
    Are Battle Born power stations safe to use indoors?

    Yes. Because they produce no fumes or emissions, they are safe for indoor use when operated as directed.

    Battle Born, powered by Dragonfly Energy, delivers high-performance LiFePO4 power solutions designed for reliability, safety, and long-term use across demanding applications.
