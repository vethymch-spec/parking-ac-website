Not as expected in a Marriott property
LOCATION: This property is strategically located near Plaza del Sol Mall and Expo facilities in Guadalajara. Not close to the city center and 30 ~ 45 minutes to airport depends on traffic. This building, built in 1973 has been using ny this hotel since 1982. Last renovation according to records in 2012.

ROOMS: As the building is rounded shape the rooms and bathrooms tend to be smaller than others in the area. Windows aren't big so the views are somehow restricted. As it is in the middle of two important avenues, expect some noise .
Ammenities are simple two bottles of water, elementary toiletterie (no showercap).
it was somehow sad and disappointing to see the towels in the room. I have been in many hotels, all categories but never had towels in such a bad shape (see pics). I don't know if it is a problem of management but it is really not expected from a chain like Marriott

RESTAURANT: check very well the rate you buy and specifically if it has breakfast included to avoid confusions. At breakfast service was low pitch and what grabbed my attention was the very dirty appron and blouse of the lady welcoming guests.

PARKING: Has free not covered parking next to the building.

Staff at front desk was kind and tried their best.

El1Lobo wrote a review Mar 2015
Granbury, Texas1,524 contribution314 helpful votes
A pleasantly different place to stay
We had never been to Guadalajara before so our 1st trip was a little challenging. The 1st thing that stuck out was that you need to bring your “A” game for language skills.
The place we were staying was a 14 story circular building attached to a mall, Plaza del Sol. It seemed small but with 14 stories and 6 units per floor it was adequate. It was booked full with lots of kids [more on that later]. Due to it being full we could not check in early and it was midafternoon before we got in. Our 1 bedroom condo consisted of an open kitchen dining and living room, master bedroom, the bathroom and a hallway with the closet and safe. Since it is a circular building the rooms were fairly large with the living room and master bedroom being on the outside of the building. That quickly addressed why there were so many kids, the room could accommodate many above the allowed assigned room occupancy. The kitchen had the minimal needed to cook, but again it was very adequate. The only thing that was a problem with the room was the lighting in the living room, there was very little. During the day with 8 foot windows it was no problem but after sunset there were NO lights in the living room except one small ceiling light. Reading anything at night was very challenging [2nd day they found us a lamp]. The other surprise was that this place has NO pool, but they have a small gym and a good size business area with printer and 2 computers. Wi Fi was included and work well. The decor is a mix of modern in the lobby area and dated in the rooms. Since we only will be using it for sleeping, it is perfect for us. And the staff is excellent. They did everything in their power to make you feel welcome. The bellman/valet are not allowed up stairs so take you luggage from the lobby to your room, but they provide carts. Since it is one of the higher buildings in the area, it was a great beacon to get us easily home daily. Parking here is strictly valet, but free [we tipped].
TV – No one comes to watch TV on vacation, but it is great to get caught up with events and just veg out after battling traffic and parking all day. They have some English speaking channels, but even that switch to Spanish or Chinese at will. Likewise some of the Spanish stations switch to English at will. You will have no ABC, NBC, CBS or USA etc. You will get some off beat channels with many on a loop for the week. Give up on news from the states and just hunt and peck the channels till you find something to occupy your down time.
Elevator - They work, but are not the fastest. The item that drove me nuts is that the displays did not always work so you never knew if the elevator was coming or going. One in the Lobby always showed “L”. The other on the inside always showed “14”.
Kitchen – It is adequate with 2 burners and a good large frying pan. Missing are things like wine glasses, some decent knives and a smaller frying pan to name a few. Luckily for us we are aware that many places do not stock their kitchens well so we travel with a small omelet pan, 5 different knives and measuring spoons. We also go out to eat a lot so this too would determine how you feel about the kitchen.
Dining room – a 4 chair table that looks good, but the table surface has inlays and a groove around the outer edge. Even though this makes the table look good, it makes it hard to get crumbs, etc. out of the grooves for cleaning.
Bathroom – It is larger than a cruise ship and functional. Since it is a circular building, the toilet is tight [closest to the center], but the sink area gets bigger and the shower is a good size. The water is great for temperature, but a little lacking in pressure.
The only problem I had was that on the last day the microwave blew the fuse. When I called down they sent someone to reset the fuse, but then they called the room rather than knock to let me know it was done. It was 0505 in the morning and my wife was still asleep. She was not happy about being woken up at 0505.
The location and the staff make this place. They were all so nice and helpful. Between my limited Spanish and their limited English we all did fine. Best of all was Jose [bellman/valet] who helped us locate places we wanted to see and showed us the best way to get around. A special thanks to Jose for making this trip so much better.
