Parking

Parking

Atlantic City International Airport has three parking options all within walking distance of the terminal.  In addition, a free cell phone lot is available for pick-up of arriving passengers. Effective January 1, 2024, ACY parking became fully cashless. (See below for payment options.) The overflow lot will only be available when the economy lot and parking garage are full.

Parking

Getting around ACY is easy, and the terminal layouts below will assist you with finding our shops, restaurants, and amenities.﻿

  • Hours of Operation

    Parking lots are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

  • Rates

    Cashless Parking


    Effective January 1, 2024, Atlantic City International Airport became fully cashless. Forms of payment accepted are Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

  • E-ZPass Plus

    Pay your parking fees with EZPass Plus - When using EZPass Plus at ACY you must enter through a designated EZPass only lane in order to exit with EZPass. Your EZPass account will be debited for fees less than $20. Parking fees greater than $20 will be charged directly to the credit card used to replenish your account. When using EZPass for parking, you will not receive a parking ticket.
