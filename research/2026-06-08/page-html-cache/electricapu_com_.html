Longest Runtime on the Market – up to 20 hours of cooling and 72 hours of heating both cab and engine, on a single charge.

Cooling:
Tests performed on a single charge in hot/humid Florida weather conditions. The unit maintained a cab temperature of 72°F for 10 hours in 99°F ambient temperatures. In temperatures like 85°F – 90°F, the unit can maintain the cab temperature of 72°F for up to 20 hours.

Heating:
The powerful coolant heater can keep the cab temperature of 70°F in ambient temperatures near zero. As it circulates hot coolant, the system maintains engine temperature of 130-140°F in near- zero ambient temperatures. And it’s able to do it for up to 72 hours in those conditions, with a consumption of around 1 gallon for 10 hours.
