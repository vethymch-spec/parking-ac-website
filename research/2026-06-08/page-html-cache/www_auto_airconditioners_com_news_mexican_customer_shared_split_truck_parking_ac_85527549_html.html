Mexican Customer Shared Split Truck Parking AC Installation: Metal Outdoor Unit & Quiet Indoor Unit

As a professional manufacturer of truck parking air conditioners, nothing makes us happier than receiving positive feedback and real installation photos from our global customers-especially from our valued partners in Mexico!

Recently, one of our Mexican customers kindly shared photos of our split type truck parking air conditioner installed on their heavy-duty truck. Seeing our products perfectly integrated into local Mexican truck fleets and bringing comfort to drivers is the greatest recognition of our quality and design.

 

Let's take a closer look at the key features of this split truck parking AC that won the trust of our Mexican customer:

 

 

1. Metal Shell Outdoor Unit: Built for Mexico's Harsh High-Temperature Conditions

 

Mexico's trucking routes often face intense sunlight and high temperatures, which put great pressure on the durability of vehicle accessories. Our split truck parking AC is equipped with a metal shell outdoor unit-unlike plastic shells that are prone to aging and damage under long-term exposure to the sun, the metal shell offers superior heat dissipation, corrosion resistance, and impact resistance. It can withstand the harsh outdoor environment of Mexican roads, ensuring stable operation even in extreme heat, and extending the service life of the AC unit.

 

 

2. Ultra-Quiet Indoor Unit: Create a Peaceful Rest Space for Drivers

 

Long-haul truckers in Mexico often need to rest in the cab during parking breaks, and noise can greatly affect their rest quality. Our split parking AC is designed with an ultra-quiet indoor unit, which operates at a low noise level that won't disturb drivers' sleep or rest. While delivering powerful cooling performance, it maintains a quiet and comfortable cab environment, helping drivers recover their strength faster for the next journey.

 

3. Engine-Off Operation & 12V/24V Compatibility: Save Fuel & Fit All Heavy-Duty Trucks

 

For Mexican truckers, fuel costs are a key concern. Our split truck parking AC runs directly on the truck's 12V or 24V battery, no need to idle the engine to keep cool. This not only saves a lot of fuel costs but also reduces engine wear and carbon emissions-aligning with the efficiency needs of Mexican truck fleets and individual drivers.

 

The split design also offers flexible installation options, suitable for all types of heavy-duty trucks common in Mexico, without damaging the original vehicle structure. It's easy to install and maintain, bringing convenience to our customers and their drivers.

 

 

We are deeply grateful for the trust and support of our Mexican customer. Every installation photo and feedback is a driving force for us to continuously improve our products. As a manufacturer dedicated to providing high-quality truck HVAC solutions, we are committed to bringing more reliable, efficient, and comfortable parking cooling solutions to truckers and fleet operators across Mexico.

 

If you are a Mexican truck fleet owner, distributor, or truck modification workshop looking for high-quality split truck parking air conditioners, feel free to contact us. We offer competitive prices, stable supply, and professional after-sales support, and look forward to building long-term win-win cooperation with more partners in Mexico!
