FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

An APU (Auxiliary Power Unit) is a device installed on a semi-truck that provides power for heating, cooling, and other electrical needs without running the main engine. Truck APUs help drivers reduce fuel consumption and comply with anti-idling laws by offering an alternative source of power during rest periods. APUs are typically available in diesel or electric configurations.

Diesel APUs have a small diesel engine that powers a generator to provide electricity, HVAC, and sometimes battery charging.

Electric APUs use high-capacity lithium batteries to run HVAC systems and other electronics without fuel.

Key differences:

  • Diesel APUs offer longer runtime but require fuel and maintenance.
  • Electric APUs are quieter, emission-free, and ideal for fleets looking to reduce idle hours and maintenance costs.

The cost of an APU varies:

  • Diesel APU: $8,000–$12,000 installed
  • Electric APU: $6,000–$10,000 installed

    Price depends on the brand, battery system, features (cooling/heating), and installation complexity. While the initial cost may seem high, APUs often pay for themselves within 12–24 months through fuel and maintenance savings.

Running your truck’s main engine while parked can burn 0.8–1 gallon of diesel per hour. APU systems can cut idling time drastically, saving drivers between $3,000 and $6,000 per year in fuel, depending on usage. Electric APUs provide even more savings by completely eliminating fuel usage during rest time.

Yes, electric APUs are increasingly popular due to lower long-term operating costs, zero emissions, quieter operation, and minimal maintenance. Modern lithium battery systems offer overnight runtimes (8–10 hours or more) and fast charging, making electric APUs a smart investment for drivers focused on efficiency and compliance.

Yes. Most advanced APU systems—both diesel and electric—offer air conditioning and heating solutions. Diesel APUs typically include integrated diesel heaters, while electric APUs often pair with 12V bunk heaters or diesel-fired heaters for cold weather performance.

An APU is not legally required, but many fleets and owner-operators choose to install one to meet anti-idle regulations, especially in states like California, New York, and Illinois, where idling laws are strictly enforced. APUs also improve driver comfort and reduce engine wear from excessive idling.

Professional APU installation takes approximately 6 to 12 hours, depending on the truck model and whether it’s a diesel or electric unit. Some manufacturers offer DIY APU kits for experienced installers looking to save on labor costs.

Most high-quality electric APUs powered by lithium batteries provide 8 to 12 hours of runtime per charge. Runtime can vary based on battery capacity, ambient temperature, and system efficiency. Dual-battery or dual-purpose starting battery setups can extend runtime significantly.

The best APU depends on your needs, but some top-rated APU systems in 2025 include:

  • Thermo King TriPac Evolution (Diesel APU)
  • Carrier ComfortPro (Diesel APU)
  • Polar Fox APU (Electric APU with lithium battery system)

Polar Fox APU, for example, stands out in the electric APU market for its simple design, lithium power, and ease of installation.
