        Hi All,

         

        I’ve spent a few hours reading articles on here, watching videos on Webasto, Eberspacher and cheap Chinese diesel water heaters and would appreciate people’s opinions on which make of heater to buy. Is there a general consensus on which brand offers the best all round water heater? 

         

        As an alternative, there are cheap Chinese alternatives (https://www.banggood.com/12V-5KW-Plumbing-Water-Heater-Kit-for-Truck-SUV-Bus-RV-Boats-p-1614469.html?p=CV092175542878202310&custlinkid=3603091&cur_warehouse=CN&ID=514829), I’m under no illusion that the quality of this is far below what you’d get with either of the other two brands. I’d also think that on top of the overall price of £310 for that kit, I’d be spending another £100-£150 on additional parts. If it lasted 2/3 years replacing it wouldn’t be a major issue, especially if you consider the overall price of  a Webasto or Eberspacher and their related servicing costs.

         

        As an additional thought I’ve also seen a heater by MV heating (https://mvheating.co.uk/product/mv-hydro-5/), this falls somewhere in between high and low price. 

         

        I don’t live on my boat, spending maybe a night on it per week during winter. It has a calorifier and 4 small radiators, so any unit isn’t going to be working as hard as it might otherwise do.

        I bought a reconditioned Webasto Thermotop 5 kit from eBay for £375 including most of the ancillaries and installed it myself about 3 years ago. I also fitted a CAV/Delphi water/sediment trap in the fuel line to the heater.

         

        Of course the usual forum advice was not to buy a recon unit from eBay, that it wasn't a marine unit and operated at different voltages, that the exhaust would leak CO and kill me, etc, etc. 

         

        In fact it's a great bit of kit that's performed well. I knew it was a unit from a road vehicle and was aware that it required slightly higher voltages but it really hasn't been an issue because I never let my batteries drop that low. As far as the exhaust goes you can seal it with a 6mm bolt or just buy a Webasto marine silencer.

         

        It's a relatively cheap solution compared to buying a new marine webasto but it's obviously a bit hit or miss depending on whether you receive a decent reconditioned unit from the eBay seller. I took a punt and it worked out.

        We had a Webasto Airtop 2000 on our boat and it was super reliable. It ran many many hours with very few problems.

         

        We had the boat 13 years and it was serviced once in that time when we had to remove it for a new fan unit fitting. Other than that it never gave any problems and we used the boat for most weekends and holidays. 

         

        We never had any problems with battery voltage with ours. They use very little power when just ticking over, it is on start up and shut down that they use more for a short period.

         

        A lot of our friends switched to the cheap chinese units after having problems with Ebbersplutters. They just carry a spare one for if it does breakdown. Although as far as im aware none of them have broken down yet.

        I watched a video by a well known and well-respected boat electrician, and he was not complimentary about Eberspacher diesel heaters (after a decade of working on them for part of his living). His recommendation was Webasto instead, or maybe Mikuni. 

        A boater friend had his Eber malfunction in May of last year and he tried everything to revive it. He stripped it, cleaned it, serviced it, replaced the glow pin, you name it. He was a tight bugger, so he probably tried to reduce the thing to its constituent molecules and reassemble it- but nothing worked and he eventually felt he had to buy a new one. 

        Mine went on the blink back in April or May (when I still needed it on some cool mornings), and I gave it to a trusted local chap who repairs them, but he couldn't fix it after a week of messing around. That was a lightly-used unit too, and about 8 years old.

        Already over £150 down, and running out of time to move my boat on, I gave up and bought a brand new one (over £900 for just the heater unit). 

        I really didnt want to get another Eber, but I wasnt sure if a Webasto would swap straight in there, and I was struggling to get hold of just the Webasto heater unit- and the full kit is pretty expensive.

        My next option was going to be a replacement air motor at almost £300, so I decided I was throwing good money after bad and went for the brand new one.
