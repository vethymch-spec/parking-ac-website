Parking Air Conditioner Supplier: 12V and 24V Mobile HVAC Systems

Direct factory supply of parking air conditioners for trucks, RVs, vans, buses and construction equipment.

Vethy is a parking air conditioner supplier serving distributors, importers, OEMs and fleet buyers across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Oceania. We supply 12V and 24V rooftop and split parking AC systems for trucks, RVs, vans, buses and construction equipment, with stock SKUs, OEM customization and a global spare-parts pipeline.

This page summarizes what makes Vethy a credible parking AC supply partner for buyers who need consistency, transparent pricing and engineering support, not just a one-time wholesale invoice.

Application coverage

One reason buyers consolidate suppliers around Vethy is that the same factory line covers most common mobile HVAC applications:

  • Long-haul trucks – sleeper cab parking AC for Class 8 and EU heavy tractors. See semi truck parking AC.
  • RVs and motorhomes – ultra-thin rooftop AC.
  • Vans and overland 4×4 – split parking AC kits.
  • Buses and coaches – higher-capacity 24V rooftop parking AC.
  • Construction and mining – IP-rated outdoor housings for dusty environments.
  • Agriculture – tractor and harvester cab AC.

Supply scope and SKUs

Stock SKUs cover the high-volume configurations. OEM customization extends the lineup to bespoke voltage, capacity and badge. See the full product hub at product overview and individual models such as VS02-PRO, VX3000SP, VK2000 ultra-thin and VM6000 Max.

Logistics and lead time

We ship EXW, FOB and CIF from Qingdao port. Typical sea freight is 25 to 40 days to most North American and EU ports, 12 to 20 days to Southeast Asia, 30 to 45 days to South America and West Africa.

Standard SKUs ship in 15 to 25 days. OEM runs ship in 35 to 50 days. A 40HQ container loads approximately 480 rooftop units depending on model.

Technical and after-sales support

Vethy provides:

  • Installation guides and wiring diagrams per model.
  • Remote technical support by email and WhatsApp during business hours.
  • Spare-parts stock for every active SKU.
  • Warranty: 24 months on the unit, 36 months on the BLDC twin-rotor compressor.

Start the conversation

Send your country, target volume, voltage and application to info@vethy.com or open the request a quote form. We will return a proposal with stock availability, pricing and lead time within 2 to 5 working days. For wholesale path see parking AC wholesale; for OEM see truck parking AC manufacturer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell to end users or only to businesses?

Vethy is a B2B parking air conditioner supplier. We sell to distributors, importers, OEMs and fleet operators. End users in served regions are referred to our local distributor.

Can I evaluate a unit before placing a stock order?

Yes. We accept single-unit sample orders for evaluation. Sample fees are creditable against a follow-up production order.

Do you private-label for distributors?

Yes, from 50 units. Distributor private-label includes custom artwork, packaging and remote control faceplate.

Are your units supplied with refrigerant pre-charged?

Yes. All Vethy parking AC units leave the factory pre-charged with R410A, R32 or R134a as specified.

Ready to spec a Vethy parking AC?

Quote requests, OEM enquiries and distributor applications are handled by the same team. Typical response time is 2 to 5 working days.
